## Public, stable names (what apps and the outside scripts import)

- Root package (unchanged names stay): plus `AGENT`, `PERSONAL`, `SHARED`, `TASK`, `TOOLS`, `URGENT`, `FILLER`.
- `crowkis.privacy` (Python) / `@crowkis/client/privacy` (Node, `src/privacy.js`): the privacy word rules apps reuse,
  by public names: `is_personal`, `is_context_dependent`, `leaks`, `abstract`, `fill`, `SLOT`,
  `CONTEXT_BOUND_THRESHOLD` (Node: `isPersonal`, `isContextDependent`, `leaks`, `abstract`, `fill`, `SLOT`,
  `CONTEXT_BOUND_THRESHOLD`). Re-exports only; P5.5 may move the internals, never these names.
- Node subpaths keep their names and point at the new files: `./voice`, `./chat`, `./session`, `./realtime`,
  `./livekit` (unchanged), `./privacy` (new). Their `*.d.ts` follow.
